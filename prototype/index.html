Grokeversity is a free, open-source, mastery-based course of study. Start with Mechanical Engineering or Introduction to AI. You learn with interactive diagrams and real math, pass strict quizzes at your own pace, and use an AI tutor (like Grok) that gives hints instead of answers.

How it works

1 · Learn visually

Interactive slides build the math one piece at a time. You drag diagrams, and each new symbol lights up the part it names.

2 · Prove mastery

Numeric problems need correct values and units (±1%). You advance only at ≥ 80%.

3 · Tutor, not cheat sheet

Paste our tutor prompt into Grok. It uses Socratic hints, makes you show your work, and grades strictly.

4 · Your pace

Nothing in the lessons is timed. Unit exams and finals are timed, like a real course.
