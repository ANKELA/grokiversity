1 · Learn visually

Nothing in the lessons is timed. Unit exams and finals are timed, like a real course.

Forces as vectors

ME 201 · Unit 1 · Concept 1 of 5 · ~5 h of study

Objectives
  • Resolve a 2D force into components and recombine them into magnitude and direction.
  • Write a 3D force as a Cartesian vector using a position vector and a unit vector.
  • Project a force onto a line using the dot product.

Components

A force has magnitude, direction and a point of application. With \(\theta\) measured counter-clockwise from \(+x\):

$$F_x = F\cos\theta,\qquad F_y = F\sin\theta,\qquad \vec R=\sum \vec F_i$$

$$|\vec R|=\sqrt{R_x^2+R_y^2},\qquad \theta_R=\operatorname{atan2}(R_y,R_x)$$

Worked example, step by step

A 600 N cable force acts at \(A(2,0,0)\) m toward \(B(-1,3,6)\) m. Write \(\vec F\) as a Cartesian vector.

$$\vec r_{AB}=B-A=(-3,\;3,\;6)\ \text{m}$$
$$|\vec r_{AB}|=\sqrt{9+9+36}=\sqrt{54}=7.348\ \text{m}$$
$$\hat u_{AB}=\frac{\vec r_{AB}}{|\vec r_{AB}|}=(-0.4082,\;0.4082,\;0.8165)$$
$$\vec F=600\,\hat u_{AB}=(-244.9\,\hat\imath+244.9\,\hat\jmath+489.9\,\hat k)\ \text{N}$$
Check: \(|\vec F|=\sqrt{244.9^2+244.9^2+489.9^2}=600\ \text{N}\) ✓. Always verify with something you didn't use.

Free-body diagrams & support reactions

ME 201 · Unit 1 · Concept 2 of 5 · ~5 h of study

Objectives
  • Isolate a body and draw every external force.
  • State the reactions supplied by common 2D and 3D supports.
  • Recognize two-force members and count unknowns vs. equations.

A planar rigid body gives exactly three independent equations:

$$\sum F_x=0,\qquad \sum F_y=0,\qquad \sum M_A=0$$

If there are more than 3 unknowns, the body is statically indeterminate: \(\;n_{\text{indet}} = n_{\text{unknowns}}-3\).

Supports cheat-sheet

Roller → 1 force ⟂ surface (push only) · Pin → \(A_x, A_y\) · Fixed → \(A_x, A_y, M_A\) · Cable → 1 tension · 3D ball-and-socket → 3 forces · 3D fixed → 3 forces + 3 couples

Equilibrium of a particle

ME 201 · Unit 1 · Concept 3 of 5 · ~6 h of study

Objectives
  • Apply \(\sum\vec F=\vec 0\) to a particle in 2D (2 equations) and 3D (3 equations).
  • Model springs (\(F=ks\)) and cables (tension only).
  • Solve simultaneous linear equations and sanity-check the signs.

For a ring held by two cables at angles \(\theta_1\) (left) and \(\theta_2\) (right) above horizontal, supporting weight \(W\):

$$\sum F_x:\ -T_1\cos\theta_1+T_2\cos\theta_2=0\qquad \sum F_y:\ T_1\sin\theta_1+T_2\sin\theta_2-W=0$$

$$\Rightarrow\quad T_1=\frac{W\cos\theta_2}{\sin(\theta_1+\theta_2)},\qquad T_2=\frac{W\cos\theta_1}{\sin(\theta_1+\theta_2)}$$
