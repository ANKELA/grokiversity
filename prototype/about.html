About Grokeversity

A free, open-source, degree-level curriculum you work through at your own pace. It is a community project, not an accredited institution, and it does not award degrees or academic credit.

How it works

1 · Learn visually

Interactive slides build the math one piece at a time. You drag diagrams, and each new symbol lights up the part it names.

2 · Prove mastery

Numeric problems need correct values and units (±1%). You advance only at ≥ 80%.

3 · Tutor, not cheat sheet

Paste our tutor prompt into Grok. It uses Socratic hints, makes you show your work, and grades strictly.

4 · Your pace

Nothing in the lessons is timed. Unit exams and finals are timed, like a real course. Finish a unit and you can earn a completion certificate — not a degree.

The tracks

Each track is a university-level curriculum, not an accredited degree. Statics Unit 1 is playable now. Introduction to AI is coming soon.

The Mechanical Engineering track

Modeled on ABET-style ME programs and MIT's Course 2 core. Statics is live; every other course is coming soon, and contributors are welcome.

The Introduction to AI track

Mapped to Stanford CS221 and CS229, MIT 6.034 and 6.036, and Berkeley CS188. This track is coming soon.

Honest disclaimer: Grokeversity is an independent, open-source community project. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by xAI or any university. It is not an accredited institution and does not award degrees or academic credit. The ME and Introduction to AI tracks are degree-equivalent curricula, and completion certificates are not degrees.
